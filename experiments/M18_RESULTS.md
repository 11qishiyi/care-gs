# M18 — does patience buy the fidelity term?

Round 14, 2026-11-15.  One scene (bicycle), one node (A100-PCIE-40GB, 2.3 h, ¥6.3).  Verdict:
**`representation_bound_confirmed`** again — 30 000 finetune steps buy 0.57 dB where 2.42 were
needed, so the M17 shortfall is budget, not patience.

## Question

M17 measured our best at HAC's 27.54 MB as 21.51 dB after 8000 finetune steps, 2.99 dB under the
pre-registered bar.  The obvious objection: 8000 steps is short — pruning papers retrain for the
full 30 k.  M18 ran the same pruning (same score, same fractions) and the same codec grid with
**30 000** steps at the two keep fractions that sit inside the published budgets.

Pre-registered at 09:25, before any data: K4 keep .35 (2.15 M primitives, ~25.6 MB) for HAC's
27.54 MB, K5 keep .25 (1.53 M primitives, ~18.2 MB) for PCGS's 19.09 MB; criterion **C6** =
best row at ≤27.54 MB reaches 24.50 dB, **C7** = best row at ≤19.09 MB reaches 24.62 dB (each
published number minus the 0.55 dB protocol bias C1 measured on this scene).

## Measured

| keep | steps | primitives | clean PSNR | ft gain | best row ≤27.54 MB | best row ≤19.09 MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| .35 | 8 000 (M17) | 2,146,184 | 21.81 | +4.93 | `vq12_c8_o8` 25.67 MB / 21.51 dB | — |
| .35 | **30 000** | 2,146,184 | **22.54** | **+5.66** | `vq12_c8_o8` 25.67 MB / **22.08 dB** | — |
| .25 | 30 000 | 1,532,988 | 21.97 | +6.79 | — | `vq12_c8_o8` 18.40 MB / **21.50 dB** |

- **C6 fails**: 22.08 dB against 24.50 — short by 2.42 dB (2.97 dB against HAC's own 25.05).
- **C7 fails**: 21.50 dB against 24.62 — short by 3.12 dB (3.62 against PCGS's 25.12).

The marginal return is the number that matters: **+0.73 dB clean / +0.57 dB coded for 22 000 extra
steps**, i.e. 0.026 dB per thousand steps, and the per-step gain is falling because the rate decays.
Closing 2.42 dB at that rate needs ~90 000 steps at this keep fraction — and the 8 k → 30 k gain
itself is already smaller than the 0 → 8 k gain (+4.93), so the curve is flattening, not linear.
Extrapolating a 30 k-from-scratch schedule (rather than a 30 k finetune of a pruned 30 k model)
would plausibly add another 0.3–0.5 dB, which still leaves ≥1.9 dB.

## What the two rows say together

Primitive count still dominates: dropping from 2.15 M to 1.53 M costs 0.57 dB clean (22.54 → 21.97)
and 0.58 dB coded, while 22 000 extra finetune steps buy 0.57 dB.  Those are the same size, which is
the whole difficulty: at this end of the range, one more doubling of training time is worth exactly
one more 40 % of the primitives, and we do not have the bytes for either.

The ceiling is visible in the base model itself: 6.13 M primitives at 24.52 dB.  A 35 % subset that
finetunes back to 22.54 dB is 1.98 dB below the model it came from, and the codec then costs another
0.46 dB.  For HAC to be matched at 27.54 MB we would need a 2.15 M-primitive model at ≈25.0 dB —
i.e. **0.5 dB above the un-pruned 6.13 M model** at a third of the primitives.  That is not a
training-schedule question.

## What changes in the paper

Nothing in the claims; one number improves.  The mid-rate row of our method becomes
**22.08 dB at 25.67 MB (19.8×)**, and the small-budget row **21.50 dB at 18.40 MB (27.6×)** — both
measured on the published protocol with our own codec, both reported with their gap to HAC and PCGS
rather than in isolation.  The near-lossless shelf (5.2× at −0.09 pp, 9.4× at −0.26 pp) remains the
only place we lead, and it is now the only place we should claim.

table and the M16/M17/M18 artifacts, so the paper's comparison table and the round reports read the

msc12-6-a1_c8_o8 at 278.50 MB is 0.62 dB behind), HAC at 27.54 MB / 25.05 dB (we are 2.97 dB
behind at 25.67 MB), PCGS at 19.09 MB / 25.12 dB (3.62 dB behind at 18.40 MB) -- plus our shelf on
the base model, which is where the near-lossless claim lives.

## Reproduction

```
bash experiments/run_m18.sh deps data base     # ~25 min on a fresh node
bash experiments/run_m18.sh k35 & bash experiments/run_m18.sh k25 &   # 63–70 min, 30 k steps each
bash experiments/run_m18.sh compress           # bytes + dumps with `clean`, ~16 min
bash experiments/run_m18.sh report             # rd json + C5-style report, ~10 min
```

Artifacts: `experiments/artifacts/a4/m18_{rd,bytes,report}.{json,md}`.  The report prints its
criterion as "C5" because it reuses the M17 bar verbatim (same budget, same 0.55 dB bias); C6/C7 in
the pre-registration are that bar applied to the two published budgets.
